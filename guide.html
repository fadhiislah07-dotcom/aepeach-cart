🍑 How to Track Your Order

a quick guide, step by step

  1. 1

    Search your Telegram username

    On the home page, type your Telegram username into the search bar (with or without the @) and tap Search.

  2. 2

    Check your order summary

    A dashboard appears showing your total orders, and how many are Secured, In Transit, Ready for Postage, Complete, or Cancelled.

  3. 3

    Browse your items

    Below the dashboard, every item you've claimed is listed by batch tag — showing the item name, quantity, and a little status tracker (🌱 Secured → 🚚 In Transit → 📦 Ready for Postage → 🍑 Complete).

  4. 4

    Filter by status

    Use the "Filter by status" dropdown under your dashboard to narrow the list down to just one status at a time — handy if you only want to see what's still In Transit, for example.

  5. 5

    Checkout when your items are ready

    If any of your items are Ready for Postage, a Checkout button appears next to the filter. Tap it to see the full list, tap 📋 Copy list to copy it, then tap Checkout → to send it to us on Telegram and arrange postage.

  6. 6

    Quick re-search next time

    Your last few searched usernames are saved as tappable chips under the search bar, so you don't have to type your username again.

  7. 7

    Switch to dark mode

    Tap the 🌙 / ☀️ icon in the top bar anytime to switch between light and dark mode. It'll remember your choice next time you visit.

  8. 8

    Read our T&C

    Tap T&C in the top bar or footer anytime to review our terms and payment guidelines before claiming.
